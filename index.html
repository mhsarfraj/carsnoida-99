How to buy a used car in Noida

The checks that matter, the papers that matter. Do these on any used car, here or anywhere else. We will sit with you through all five.

1. Ask for the service history

Look for the last major service, timing belt or chain, and clutch. A gap of two years in the log book is worth asking about.

2. Match the RC

Chassis and engine numbers on the RC should match the ones stamped on the car. Check the owner count on the RC against what the listing says.

3. Confirm insurance and NOC

Check the policy expiry and the no-claim bonus. If the car is registered outside UP, an NOC from the original RTO is needed before transfer.

4. Run a challan check

Search the registration number on the UP or Delhi traffic police site. Pending challans follow the car, not the previous owner.

5. Do a proper PDI

Sight the body panels, check tyre date codes, test all electricals and the AC, and take a drive over a speed breaker.

Selling: common questions

Clear answers about selling your used car

How soon do I get paid for my car?

Instantly. The full amount is credited to your bank account via IMPS/RTGS on the spot before you hand over the keys and sign the delivery receipt.

What about RC transfer and driving liability?

We issue a formal legal Delivery Receipt and Sale Letter immediately upon handover, transferring all liability from you. We manage the RTO transfer free of cost.

Can I sell a car with an active bank loan?

Yes! We settle the outstanding loan amount directly with your lending bank and pay the remaining balance to your account.

What if my car is near 10/15 year NGT limit?

We facilitate state RTO NOCs for re-registration outside Delhi-NCR, ensuring you receive top resale value.
